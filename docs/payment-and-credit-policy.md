# SkillSense — Payment, Credit System & Policy Information

Welcome to SkillSense. This document provides clear, transparent information regarding how payments are processed, our refund policy, and how our credit system works. Please review these details before purchasing assessment credits.

---

## 1. Payment Gateway & Security

* **Payment Processor:** We use **Razorpay** as our official payment gateway partner for processing online transactions (UPI, Debit/Credit Cards, Net Banking, and Wallets).
* **Secure Transactions:** All transactions are routed directly through Razorpay's encrypted and PCI-DSS compliant checkout interface.
* **Credential Safety:** SkillSense does not store your banking passwords, card CVVs, or sensitive payment credentials. All platform gateway keys and communication credentials are managed securely through server-side environment configurations.

---

## 2. Refund Policy

* **No Refund Policy:** All credit purchases, subscription plans, and promotional redemptions on SkillSense are **final and non-refundable**.
* **Instant Digital Delivery:** Because assessment credits are digital goods that are added to your wallet immediately upon verified payment, we do not offer refunds, chargebacks, or cash exchanges once a transaction has completed.
* **Assistance:** If you experience a billing anomaly (such as being charged without receiving your purchased credits), please contact platform support with your transaction details so we can investigate and credit your wallet.

---

## 3. How the Credit System Works

We designed our credit system to be straightforward, fair, and flexible for students:

* **1 Assessment Credit = 1 Full Career Roadmap Unlock:**
  Each credit unlocks the complete, personalized Career Roadmap and comprehensive AI guidance report for one specific assessment attempt.
* **Free Assessment & Teaser:**
  * Taking the 42-question RIASEC career assessment is completely **free**.
  * Completing the assessment does not consume any credits.
  * Viewing your top career match teaser summary is free and does not consume credits.
* **Permanent Unlock:**
  Spending 1 credit permanently unlocks that specific assessment attempt. Once unlocked, you can revisit and review your full roadmap anytime from your account dashboard without ever spending another credit.
* **Independent Assessments:**
  Each assessment attempt is tracked independently. Unlocking one assessment does not unlock a different or subsequent assessment attempt.
* **Purchasing Credits:**
  Credits can be purchased directly from the **Pricing** page according to your needs (e.g., Single Assessment Unlock or Multi-Credit bundles).
* **Referral & Campaign Discounts:**
  Valid school, workshop, or promotional referral codes reduce the payable price of a credit plan at checkout. Discounts affect the purchase price only and do not alter the standard 1 credit per assessment roadmap mapping unless explicitly designated as a promotional credit grant.

By proceeding with a purchase or applying credits on SkillSense, you acknowledge and agree that:

1. You understand that online payments are processed securely via **Razorpay**.
2. You understand and accept that credit purchases are **non-refundable**.
3. You understand that **1 credit unlocks 1 specific assessment's full career roadmap permanently**, while taking tests and viewing teaser results remains free.
